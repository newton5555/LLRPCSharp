v2.0.4 release notes

  • Keeps GPO output updates configuration-only so an output change does not redeploy managed inventory resources.
  • Recognizes missing-resource responses from common LLRP 1.0.1 and Zebra readers and adds regression coverage.
  • Keeps LLRP 1.0.1, 1.1 and 2.0 SDK baselines; physical 2.0 interoperability remains separately verified.
  • Publishes LlrpSdk, Impinj, Zebra and LlrpDevice.Virtual.Hosting packages at v2.0.4.
